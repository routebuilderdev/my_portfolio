outcomeSummary: "Built an open-source European job aggregator with an opinionated scoring engine — eight hard gates, verified net pay, real micro-town rent, work-life-balance penalties"
overview: "An open-source, self-hosted European job aggregator that does not compete on volume. Every job in the feed is reduced to one number — monthly savings — computed from verified net pay, real micro-town rent, and baseline living costs, then gated by eight hard personal filters and a work-life-balance score. Built entirely with AI assistance."
problem: "Generic job boards answer 'what jobs exist?'. A professional driver relocating to Europe asks a different question: 'how much do I save per month, and will I hate my life?' Nothing on the market answers that — aggregators compete on volume, not on trusted, opinionated decision support."
constraints:
  - "Must answer one question: 'How much do I save per month, and will I hate my life?'"
  - "Zero budget — every number on a Job Card traceable to a source and date, €0/month infrastructure on one VPS"
  - "No paid service in the core stack — every paid tool named and replaced with an OSS alternative"
  - "Must work for a specific locked default persona (European coach/bus driver) plus data-driven alternative personas"
  - "Open source (MIT), self-hosted"
  - "Built entirely with AI assistance"
approach: "Designed the data pipeline, schema, and API before writing code — the complete developer-ready build spec with real SQL, real interfaces, and golden test fixtures came first. Built a FastAPI backend with SQLAlchemy 2.0 async, Alembic migrations, and an APScheduler worker running scrapers with a €0 anti-bot strategy and JSON-LD extraction. The frontend is a React 18 + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind single-page app. A persona-aware Scoring Engine ranks every job through eight hard gates to a Tier A/B/C rating, with an honesty rule that refuses to claim a confidence it cannot prove."
keyDecisions:
  - decision: "Opinionated scoring engine over pure aggregation"
    reasoning: "The wedge is not 'more jobs than Indeed' — Indeed already wins that war. The wedge is decision support: eight hard gates reject silently, net pay is verification, rents are measured, and the feed is short instead of noisy. The product is allowed to say no."
    alternatives:
      - "Volume-led aggregation competing with Indeed"
      - "Ranked stars based on user ratings"
  - decision: "One number as the product — monthly savings"
    reasoning: "Every job card reduces to a single, traceable number: verified net pay minus real micro-town rent minus baseline living costs, then penalized by a work-life-balance score. One number is honest, comparable, and impossible to fake with ranking games."
    alternatives:
      - "Multiple competing scores per listing"
      - "Keyword-match relevance ranking"
  - decision: "Data-driven personas instead of one fixed audience"
    reasoning: "The locked default persona (coach_bus_default) is campaign-verified. Three further data-driven personas — truck transition, UK depot, cross-border commuter — score the same job with their own gates. The default never moves, but the engine can serve more than one driver."
    alternatives:
      - "Hardcode the single campaign persona forever"
      - "Generic non-persona scoring"
  - decision: "Zero-cost by design, scaling on triggers"
    reasoning: "Infrastructure is one VPS and €0/month. Future growth modules (paid SaaS tiers, cross-border tax engine beyond CH-IT) are specified but not built until a defined trigger fires — no serverless platform ever enters the core stack."
    alternatives:
      - "Serverless infra from day one"
      - "Premature SaaS monetization"
